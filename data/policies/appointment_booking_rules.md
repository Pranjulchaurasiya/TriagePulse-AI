# St. Jude Medical Centre - Appointment Booking & Clinical Triage Rules

## Appointment Categories
1. **Urgent Same-Day Triage Appointments**:
   - For acute medical problems that cannot safely wait 24 hours (e.g., sudden severe earache, acute asthma flare-up not requiring 999, high fever in young children, acute urinary infections).
   - Same-day duty doctor telephone triage call within 2 hours. Face-to-face appointment offered if clinically indicated.
   - Patients must phone between 08:00 and 11:30 for same-day triage.

2. **Routine Doctor Consultations**:
   - For chronic condition management, medication reviews, persistent mild symptoms, or administrative certificates.
   - Bookable up to 2 weeks in advance.
   - Typical slots: 10 minutes face-to-face or 10 minutes telephone consultation.

3. **Practice Nurse & HCA Clinics**:
   - Routine blood tests (phlebotomy): 08:30 to 12:30 daily (morning fasting slots available).
   - Childhood immunisations, travel vaccinations, cervical smears, wound dressing, and suture removal.
   - Asthma and COPD annual reviews with respiratory nurse.

4. **Home Visits**:
   - Strictly reserved for bedbound, palliative, or severely frail patients who cannot leave their home.
   - Requests must be received by 10:30 am for same-day visit assessment.

## Booking Rules & Restrictions
- The AI receptionist may only offer open, unassigned slots matching the patient's urgency criteria.
- Do NOT prescribe or promise specific medication.
- If symptoms represent an acute red flag (chest pain, acute breathing difficulty, stroke symptoms), DO NOT offer an appointment; divert immediately to 999/A&E.
