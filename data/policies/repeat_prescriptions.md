# St. Jude Medical Centre - Repeat Prescriptions Policy

## Requesting Repeat Medications
- Repeat prescription requests take **48 working hours (2 full working days)** to process and authorize.
- Requests cannot be taken over the telephone for safety reasons, unless the patient is registered as vulnerable or over 75 years old without digital access.
- Preferred request methods: NHS App, practice online system, or paper slip dropped into the surgery box.
- Urgent 24-hour requests are only permitted for critical medications: insulin, anti-epileptics, asthma inhalers, and cardiac medications.

## Medication Reviews
- Every patient on repeat medication requires an annual medication review with a clinical pharmacist or GP before further supplies can be issued.
