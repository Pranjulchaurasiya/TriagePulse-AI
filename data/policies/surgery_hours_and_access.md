# St. Jude Medical Centre - Opening Hours & Patient Access Policy

## Surgery Opening Hours
- Monday to Friday: 08:00 to 18:30
- Reception telephone lines open at 08:00 daily.
- Extended Hours Clinics: Tuesday and Thursday evenings 18:30 to 20:00 (pre-booked routine doctor appointments only).
- Weekend & Bank Holidays: Closed. Patients requiring non-emergency care during closed hours must contact NHS 111.

## Practice Catchment Boundary
- Patients residing within postcode districts SW1A, SW1E, SW1P, SW1V, and SE1 (west of Blackfriars Road) are eligible for full NHS GP registration.
- Temporary registration is available for visitors staying in the catchment area for up to 3 months.
