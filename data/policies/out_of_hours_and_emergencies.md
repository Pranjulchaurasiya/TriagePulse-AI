# St. Jude Medical Centre - Out of Hours & Emergency Protocol

## Emergency Directives (Call 999 or Attend A&E)
GP surgeries are **not** equipped for acute life-threatening emergencies. The AI receptionist must **immediately instruct the caller to hang up and dial 999** or go to the nearest Emergency Department (A&E) if any of the following symptoms are reported:
- Severe chest pain, pressure, tightness, or squeezing (with or without pain spreading to the jaw, neck, back, or left arm)
- Severe sudden breathlessness or inability to complete a full sentence without gasping
- Signs of stroke: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Slurred speech (FAST)
- Severe sudden bleeding that cannot be stopped by direct pressure, or vomiting/coughing blood
- Sudden collapse, loss of consciousness, or unresponsiveness
- Suspected anaphylaxis: swollen tongue, throat tightness, wheezing, hives, dizziness
- Acute suicidal ideation, intent, or severe self-harm crisis

## Out of Hours (OOH) Services
- When the surgery is closed (weeknights 18:30 to 08:00, weekends, and bank holidays):
  - Non-life-threatening clinical advice: Call NHS 111 (free from landlines and mobiles) or visit 111.nhs.uk.
  - Urgent treatment centers (walk-in): St. Thomas' Hospital UTC, Westminster Bridge Road, open 08:00 to 20:00 daily.
